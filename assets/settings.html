Save browsing history
Pages you visit in tabs and Little Eclipse windows are kept on this Mac for 90 days, for History (⌘Y) and the command bar's suggestions. When off, nothing new is added; what is already saved stays until you clear it or it is 90 days old.

Max

Tidy Tabs
Shows the broom beside New Tab in the sidebar whenever a Space has more than six tabs. Tidy groups tabs into categories (Development, Media, …) or by site, and closes duplicates and blank new tabs (⇧⌘T brings them back one at a time); ⇧⌘Y tidies at any time.
Searching the web…
Instant Links
Press Shift+Return on a search in the command bar (⌘K) or the address field to go straight to the top result. Separate searches with commas to open each in its own tab (up to six). Uses DuckDuckGo's "!ducky", whatever your search engine.
5-Second Previews
Hold Shift while pointing at a link for a moment to preview it in a Little Eclipse window; the next preview replaces it, and ⌘O moves it into a tab.
Design Book
Tidy Tab Titles
Pinned tabs and new favorites drop the site's name and unread counts from their titles ("(3) Inbox - Gmail" becomes "Inbox"). Rename any tab with "Rename Tab" in the command bar.

Tidy Downloads
Files with meaningless names (download.pdf, 387494876.pdf, long hashes) are renamed after the page they came from, or the site and date. An existing file is never replaced.
ChatGPT Ask…
ChatGPT in the Command Bar
Type "chatgpt" or "claude" in the command bar and press Tab to ask a question. It opens chatgpt.com or claude.ai in a new tab, and nothing is sent until you press Return.

Max runs inside Eclipse: no page, title or file name is sent to an AI service.
