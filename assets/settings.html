Eclipse Engine Settings

Ultra-minimalist WebKit browser engineered with Rust.

Real-Time Customization

Press ⌘, or click the ⚙ Settings icon on the bottom sidebar to adjust themes, accent corona colors, tab width, ad blocking shields, and memory hibernation in real time.

Apple FairPlay Streaming (4K HDR DRM)

Eclipse directly utilizes Apple's native AVFoundation and Secure Enclave hardware pipeline for encrypted media. Netflix, Apple TV+, and Amazon Prime Video stream up to 4K HDR and Dolby Vision natively with zero CPU overhead.

Hardware DRM Ready

RAM Optimization & Tab Sleeping

Unlike Chromium or standard Safari, Eclipse drops the underlying NSView and memory buffers for background tabs that stay inactive, maintaining a lean process footprint under 50 MB.
